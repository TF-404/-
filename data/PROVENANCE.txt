counties-10t.json
Source package: taiwan-atlas 2021.9.20
https://github.com/dkaoster/taiwan-atlas
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/taiwan-atlas@2021.9.20/counties-10t.json

The file is an unmodified TopoJSON redistribution of the Taiwan Ministry of
the Interior shapefile 直轄市、縣市界線 (TWD97 經緯度). Geometry is unprojected
WGS84, quantized and topologically simplified by taiwan-atlas.

GeoJSON extracted, unmodified, for three townships:
屏東縣琉球鄉 (drawn as 小琉球), 臺東縣綠島鄉 (綠島), 臺東縣蘭嶼鄉 (蘭嶼).
The page drops the matching polygons out of 屏東縣 and 臺東縣 so those
counties are the remainder. Same license as counties-10t.json.
